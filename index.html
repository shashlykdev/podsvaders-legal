Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Pods Vaders ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile game.

Information We Collect

1. Device Identifier

We collect your device's vendor identifier (a unique ID assigned to your device by Apple) to:

  • Associate your scores with your device on the global leaderboard
  • Allow you to update your leaderboard entry from the same device
  • Prevent duplicate entries from the same device

2. Username

When you submit a score to the leaderboard, you provide a username of your choice. This username is:

  • Displayed publicly on the global leaderboard
  • Stored on our servers alongside your score

3. Game Scores

Your game scores are submitted to our leaderboard server when you choose to submit them after a game ends.

Camera and Motion Data

Important: The App uses your device's camera (for face tracking) and motion sensors (for AirPods head tracking) solely for gameplay controls. This data is:

  • Processed entirely on your device
  • Never transmitted to our servers
  • Never stored or recorded
  • Used only in real-time for game controls

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Maintain a global leaderboard of high scores
  • Display your username and score to other players
  • Prevent abuse of the leaderboard system

Data Storage and Security

Leaderboard data is stored on secure servers provided by Vercel and Neon (PostgreSQL database). We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties. Your username and score are publicly visible on the leaderboard within the App.

Data Retention

Leaderboard entries are retained indefinitely unless you request deletion. Device identifiers are stored only in association with leaderboard entries.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your leaderboard entry by contacting us. Note that once deleted, your score cannot be recovered.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The only personal information collected is a self-chosen username for the leaderboard.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
